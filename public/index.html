How it works

From trade to ash in five steps

A trade happens

Anyone buys or sells $LiLa.GOV on pump.fun. On the bonding curve, 0.300 % of every trade is the creator fee.

Source: pump.fun/docs/fees (20 May 2026)

The fee lands in the vault

Creator fees accumulate in the coin's on-chain creator vault. Nobody spends them by hand.

The Director collects and splits

Every minute, as soon as the burn budget covers the minimum of 0.02 SOL, the bot collects the vault. Half goes to the buy-and-burn budget, half is paid to the lab treasury wallet. Both with receipts.

Buy on the open market

The bot buys $LiLa.GOV at market price with the burn budget, like any other buyer.

Burn, with receipt

The bought tokens are burned with the standard SPL burn instruction. The signature is posted to the feed.

Plain answers

Questions people ask

Does burning make the price go up?

Burning reduces the supply. Price is set by buyers and sellers, and nothing here guarantees anything. Liberty Labs is a memecoin. You can lose everything you put in.

How much actually gets burned?

Half of what the fees bring in. On the bonding curve the creator fee is 0.300 % of each trade (pump.fun docs, 20 May 2026). $100,000 of volume therefore buys and burns about $150 of $LiLa.GOV and pays about $150 to the lab. After graduation to PumpSwap the creator fee depends on market cap, between 0.050 % and 0.950 %.

Where does the other half go?

To the lab treasury wallet, shown under Proof. It pays for servers, artwork, marketing and the people running the lab. Every payout is an on-chain transfer you can see in the feed. Nothing is hidden and nothing is promised.

Who controls the wallets?

The Director bot holds the creator wallet key on a server; the treasury wallet belongs to the team. The bot's rules are public and tested, its code has no sell function, and every action leaves a receipt. This is still a person's server, not a smart contract. Judge it by the receipts.

How often does it burn?

Every minute, as long as the burn budget covers at least 0.02 SOL. Below that, network fees would eat a large part of the burn, so the bot waits until enough has come in. With little volume that can mean hours between burns; with heavy volume it burns every minute. The countdown in the corner shows the next check.

What about the team's own tokens?

The team bought at launch like everyone else. Those tokens are held in the public treasury wallet, shown under Proof, so anyone can see how many there are at any time. They are not locked and they are not part of the burn program. The bot only burns what it buys with fees.

Is this connected to the US government?

No. Liberty Labs is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any government, agency, official or public figure. The flags are a theme.
